Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 15, 2026

TechFuel HQ (“we,” “us,” or “our”), operated by Lowell K. Wood IV from St. Louis County, Missouri, runs the website at techfuelhq.com. This policy explains how visitor information is handled.

Information collected automatically

When you visit the site, hosting infrastructure may collect IP address, browser type and version, pages visited and time on each page, referring website, and device type and screen resolution. This information is used in aggregate to understand traffic patterns and improve content.

Information you provide

If you contact us by email, we receive the information you choose to include — typically a name, email address, and message body. This information is used only to respond to your message.

Newsletter

The site offers an email newsletter. If you subscribe, your email address is collected and processed by Buttondown (Buttondown, LLC), the newsletter service this site uses, and is used solely to send you the newsletter. Your address is never sold, shared for advertising, or used for any other purpose. Every issue contains a one-click unsubscribe link, and unsubscribing removes your address from the active list; you can also request deletion of your address entirely by emailing the contact address below. Buttondown’s own handling of subscriber data is described in the Buttondown privacy policy. Your address is retained only while you remain subscribed.

Cookies and similar technologies

The Google AdSense rows below describe what cookies and data those services may use when advertising is served on the site, so the policy is documented in advance regardless of advertising state at any given moment.

The site uses cookies and similar technologies for analytics and, where applicable, advertising. Specifically:

  • Google Analytics may set cookies to measure site usage in aggregate
  • Google AdSense (when enabled) may serve ads on the site. Google and its advertising partners use cookies to serve ads based on a visitor’s prior visits to this site and other sites. You can opt out of personalized advertising at Google’s Ads Settings. Third-party vendors, including Google, use the DoubleClick DART cookie to serve ads.
  • Grow by Mediavine loads on every page of this site today. Grow is an audience-engagement layer operated by Mediavine; its script (faves.grow.me) may set cookies or browser storage to recognize returning visitors and measure engagement across sessions. It is active regardless of whether display advertising is being served.

You can control cookie behavior through your browser settings. Advertising and personalization cookies are governed by Google Consent Mode v2, which defaults them to denied for visitors in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland until consent is granted. Before personalized advertising is served to those regions, a Google-certified consent management platform will be deployed to collect consent.

Third-party services

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (whose origin is served through the Fastly edge network) and delivered through the Cloudflare content delivery network (which proxies and caches the site at the edge). Email sent to this site’s contact address is routed by Cloudflare Email Routing to the site author’s inbox. These services may collect technical information as described in their respective privacy policies:

  • GitHub Privacy Statement
  • Cloudflare Privacy Policy
  • Fastly Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy & Terms
  • Buttondown Privacy Policy (newsletter delivery, if you subscribe)
  • Mediavine Legal and Privacy Center (Grow audience-engagement script, loaded on every page)

Children’s privacy

TechFuel HQ is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (including the EU/UK under GDPR and California under CCPA), you may have the right to access the personal data we hold about you, request correction or deletion, restrict or object to certain processing, and opt out of personalized advertising. To exercise any of these rights, email hello@techfuelhq.com.

International transfers

If you visit from outside the United States, note that information collected through the site may be transferred to and processed in the United States.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the site evolves. The “Last updated” date at the top reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the site after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

Privacy questions go to hello@techfuelhq.com.

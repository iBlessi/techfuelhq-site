What earns a place here

  1. Traceable origin. A measurement comes from our bench, or a compilation row names the page it came from.
  2. Raw access. Downloadable JSON or CSV accompanies the readable explanation whenever a standalone file exists.
  3. Reproducible method. Hardware, settings, dates, and field definitions are documented beside the result.
  4. Visible corrections. Versions and changes stay attached to the dataset instead of being silently overwritten.

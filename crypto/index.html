Crypto, from the hardware side

This section is the part of crypto TechFuelHQ is actually qualified to write about: not price predictions, not trading, and not which token to buy, but the hardware and self-hosting underneath it. If it involves a CPU, a GPU, a node running on your homelab, or a device that holds your keys, that is our lane. If it involves guessing where the price goes next, it is not.

It is the same first-party, source-cited, “what actually works” approach as the rest of the site, pointed at the machines that run the network.
